- Source repository: [chipsalliance/rocket-chip](https://github.com/chipsalliance/rocket-chip)

- Retrieved: 2026-09-10

## Failure mechanism

At the affected parent, Rocket DCache treated every nonzero LR/SC countdown
as an active reservation:

```scala
val lrscValid = lrscCount > 0
```

The same signal blocked incoming TileLink B-channel Probes:

```scala
val block_probe =
  releaseInFlight ||
  lrscValid ||
  (s2_valid_hit && s2_lr)
```

A successful LR could repeatedly rearm `lrscCount`. A continuous LR/SC
stream could therefore keep `lrscValid` asserted, hold `tl_out.b.ready` low,
and prevent a Probe from entering the DCache. The result was unbounded
coherence backpressure rather than an ordinary reservation failure.

## Necessary multi-client trigger

1. Hart A repeatedly executes LR/SC operations and continually rearms its
   reservation countdown.
2. Hart B performs a conflicting store, upgrade, or miss to the same cache
   line.
3. The shared coherence manager sends a TileLink B-channel Probe to Hart A.
4. Under the affected RTL, Hart A's DCache keeps `lrscValid` high and does
   not accept the Probe, so Hart B's coherence transaction cannot complete.

The external Probe is generated by another coherent client. A standalone
single-hart LR/SC loop cannot produce that cross-client Probe requirement.

## Canonical fix and closure

The direct upstream fix changes the reservation-active test to include a
backoff interval:

```scala
val lrscValid = lrscCount > lrscBackoff
```

and allows the countdown to continue while it is nonzero:

```scala
when (lrscCount > 0) {
  lrscCount := lrscCount - 1
}
```

The DCache consequently exposes Probe admission during the backoff window,
ensuring that a coherent peer receives forward progress even under repeated
LR traffic. The fix also updates reservation invalidation in the relevant
DCache implementations. Commit `7871ec82...` is the canonical mainline
landing commit and its direct parent is the exact affected revision
`71eaed7d60a0af6611b18fb6b33961065debae73`.
